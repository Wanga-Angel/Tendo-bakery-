How this website works

How do I prepare an order?

Choose flavours and quantities on the Category page, add instructions on the Custom page, and review your selections in your order draft.

Does saving a draft place an order?

No. Browser selections are drafts only. Send an enquiry and wait for the bakery to confirm prices, availability and arrangements. This website does not take payment.
